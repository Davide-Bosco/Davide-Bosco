I’m Davide Bosco, an R&D Specialist and Embedded Software Engineer with a Master’s degree in ICT from the University of Turin. I work at the intersection of low-level firmware development, hardware-software integration, and industrial automation.

Currently, my focus is on designing, developing, and debugging production-grade C/C++ firmware on 32-bit architectures (ESP32-S3 via ESP-IDF and FreeRTOS). My day-to-day work involves managing time-critical routines, power stage actuation (solenoid matrices and stepper motors), serial communication buses (UART, SPI, I2C), and hardware-level troubleshooting on the testbench. Alongside embedded systems, I integrate service robotics navigation platforms (SLAM/LIDAR SDKs) and build automated data pipelines.

I’m open to collaborations and opportunities in embedded firmware engineering (bare-metal & RTOS), industrial IoT, microcontrollers (ARM Cortex-M, ESP32), and electromechanical control systems. Outside of engineering, I bring the same continuous discipline and focus I apply in the gym to my development practice.
